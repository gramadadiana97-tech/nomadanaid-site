The Brief

BrandSkin is a creative agency. I'm the creative lead across its clients, so the work looks nothing alike from one project to the next. Three delivered projects show the range.

01 · A Channel, Built From Zero

The channel is Sănătate Intimă. It came in with no format and no visual system, and I built the creative end to end: editing, structure, color grading, audio, the cut and pre-cut rhythm, plus the reel system, hooks, how each one is cut, and the covers.
